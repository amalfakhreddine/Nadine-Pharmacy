# Daman & insurance customer card

A fifth service card was added to the customer page. It opens an information window explaining that customers with Daman or other insurance may not need to pay the full covered price, but approval can fail or require additional documents.

The window includes a WhatsApp button to +961 71 979 118 with a prepared message requesting a coverage check before visiting the parapharmacy.

No Firebase or Firestore rules changes are required.
